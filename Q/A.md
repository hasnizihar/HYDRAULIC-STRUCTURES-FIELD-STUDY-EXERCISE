#### 4.1.3 Apron under the Spill Gate Downstream (Chute Blocks & Stilling Basin Floor)

## 6. Next Engineering Stage: ROUND 2D & 2E — Hydraulic Calculation Framework

Since both structures were physically inspected during dry / non-spilling periods, the assignment requires us to estimate the **design safe hydraulic capacity (Discharge $Q$ and Head $H$)** that governed the engineering sizing of these structures.

### 6.1 Site A Calculation Model: Unnichchai Curved Ogee Spillway & USBR Type III Stilling Basin
1. **Spillway Discharge Formulation:**
   $$Q = C_d \cdot L \cdot H_e^{3/2}$$
   - Where:
     - $L$: Effective curved spillway crest length (derived from survey/satellite: $\approx 120.0\text{ m}$).
     - $C_d$: Discharge coefficient for standard curved ogee crest ($\\approx 2.15\text{ m}^{1/2}/\text{s}$ or $3.90\text{ Imperial}$).
     - $H_e$: Design surcharge head over crest ($H = \text{MWL} - \text{FSL} = 31.50 - 30.18 = 1.32\text{ m}$).
2. **Energy Dissipation (USBR Type III Stilling Basin):**
   - Determination of incoming Froude Number $Fr_1 = \frac{v_1}{\sqrt{g y_1}}$.
   - Conjugate (sequent) depth calculation: $y_2 = \frac{y_1}{2} \left( \sqrt{1 + 8 Fr_1^2} - 1 \right)$.
   - Head loss and energy dissipation efficiency across baffle blocks: $\Delta E = \frac{(y_2 - y_1)^3}{4 y_1 y_2}$.

### 6.2 Site B Calculation Model: Double Swiss Gate Bifurcation (Orifice / Sluice Underflow)
1. **Sluice Gate Underflow Formulation (Free / Submerged Orifice):**
   $$Q = C_d \cdot A \cdot \sqrt{2 g H}$$
   - Where:
     - $A = 2 \times (b \cdot w) = 2 \times (1.85\text{ m} \times 1.35\text{ m}) = 5.00\text{ m}^2$ (for twin gate bays).
     - $H$: Design operating hydraulic head difference across the gate (upstream canal normal supply depth minus downstream water depth $\approx 1.0\text{ to }1.2\text{ m}$).
     - $C_d$: Discharge coefficient for vertical slide gate with sharp lower edge ($C_d \approx 0.60\text{ to }0.62$).
2. **Open-Channel Delivery Capacity (Manning's Equation for downstream branch canal):**
   $$Q = \frac{1}{n} A R^{2/3} S_0^{1/2}$$
   - Verifying that downstream canal geometry can safely convey the gate discharge without overtopping embankments.
