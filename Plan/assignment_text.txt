ASSIGNMENT: 
 
Each student needs to visit* at least two locations (at least 3 kilometers apart), where they have 
closely observed Hydraulic  (water) Structures (at least one each at each location).   One 
structure should be a very common structure and the other should not.  The structures should 
deal with freshwater. 
 
For Each Structure: 
1) Take photographs (digital/clear resolution at postcard size) of the structure: Overall 
structure and key components related to Hydraulics (Water) and make sketches with 
approximate dimensions (elevations, plan, 3D view, sections, etc.) 
2) Proof that you have visited the structure (a photograph with you and the structure and 
recognizable surrounding features) 
3) Proof that you have visited the structure after the date mentioned by the lecturer. 
4) Provide Coordinates of the Structure  
5) Provide a Google extract of approximately 1:50,000 or a digital 1: 50,000 topo -sheet 
extract 
6) Describe the Hydraulic engineering purpose of the structure 
7) How are the aspects of (i) safety of the structure and (ii) safety of the environment 
looked after when you observe from the point of view of hydraulics? 
8) What are the quantity and the head of water that (in your opinion) has been used for the 
design of the structure? 
9) Provide justification for your opinion above 
10) Submit the Hydraulic structure report (both hard copy and digital) 
11) Download and show a picture of a similar structure from an internet reference and 
provide the URL with the date and time of visit to that site.  
*Note: All students are strictly advised to follow and adhere to all health and safety guidelines, 
especially those related to Dengue/Viral flu /Rat Fever (L eptospirosis), etc., as well as, 
precautions against slips, trips and falls .  If it is unsafe to visit structure locations physically 
(due to specific health or other constraints), you are allowed to use secondary data, online 
resources, etc. (with prior approval and adequate justification) , to complete this assignment  
(prior approval should be obtained).

CE4023 Hydraulic Design - Hydraulic Structures Field Study Exercise 
- Additional Notes 
Assignment or the Problem 
Visit at least two locations (at least 3 kilometers apart), and closely observe Hydraulic (water) 
Structures (at least one each at each location) which are to be associated with freshwater.  One 
structure should be a very common structure and the other should not be a common structure 
type.  Submit a “Hydraulic Struct ure Field Identification” report providing at least the 
following.   
Provide a detailed map of the location. Provide the coordinates of each location.  Photographs 
showing the entire structure and indicating  important components such as inlets, conveyance 
section, energy dissipater, and outlet.  Describe the type and purpose (hydraulic functionality) 
of the structures.  Describe how the following aspects are taken care of (i) sustainability of the 
structure and (ii) sustainability of the surrounding environment  
With appropriate justifications, make an estimate of the safe limits of the quantity and the head 
of water for each observed structure.  Compare the observed structures with those of literature 
(you may use internet sources) and comment on the differences.  
The report should be submitted both in digital and hardcopy format (digital format preferred).
